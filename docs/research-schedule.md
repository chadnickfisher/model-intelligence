# Research schedule

This is the planned public research cadence. Recurring execution is currently
paused; publishing the schedule does not mean an automated researcher is running.
Each published change retains its actual source inspection dates and limitations.

| Day | Model creators and services |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | OpenAI |
| Tuesday | Anthropic and xAI |
| Wednesday | Google / DeepMind |
| Thursday | Qwen, DeepSeek, Moonshot, MiniMax and Z.ai |
| Friday | Mistral, Cohere and AI21 |
| Saturday | Meta, Microsoft and NVIDIA |
| Sunday | Black Forest Labs, ElevenLabs, and shared inference and compute services |

These are research groups, not affiliations or claims that models are interchangeable.
Every day covers three tracks for its assigned group:

1. **Onboarding:** discover new models, providers and products, resolve exact
   identities, and establish evidence before adding supported access routes.
2. **Gaps and low confidence:** revisit uncertain task judgments and investigate
   missing evidence, contradictions, configurations, costs and eligibility.
3. **Accuracy:** check existing capabilities, benchmark setups, access, prices,
   limits and dated behavior, including regressions and reported fixes.

Model-specific offers are checked with the model creator's group. Shared provider
policies belong to Sunday, including Azure / Foundry, Bedrock / SageMaker, Google
Cloud, Hugging Face, Together, Fireworks, Groq, Cerebras, Novita, Featherless,
OpenRouter and OpenCode's hosted products. Discovery extends beyond this roster.
Coverage includes managed and serverless inference, gateways, inference
subscriptions, dedicated endpoints, and GPU or accelerator compute services.

Creator, client, host, product and service tier remain separate. For example,
OpenCode's client, Zen gateway and Go subscriptions are distinct products.
Token rates, subscription fees and instance-hour prices retain their own units;
they are not interchangeable cost comparisons. Provider behavior does not become
a model-quality rating.

New releases and material out-of-day changes can enter the next research pass;
unfinished investigations carry forward with their gaps visible. A discovery is
not a completed onboarding. The cadence does not promise that every field is
verified every week or refresh dates for records whose sources were not inspected.
Research remains public-source only, following the [methodology](../methodology.md)
and [research coverage ledger](../data/research-coverage.yaml).
