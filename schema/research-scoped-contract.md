# Scoped public-source research contract

`research-scoped-batch.schema.json` extends the existing model batch workflow with
direct provider research and separately pinned onboarding. Existing model batches
retain their format. The research agent supplies source inspections and judgments;
the compiler validates structure, identities, accounting and repository conflicts
without inference calls. Structural acceptance cannot certify source adequacy.

The trusted assignment pins its public Git baseline, canonical state, task rubric,
receiver/schema hashes, subjects and bounds. A subject is an existing provider to
review, a resolved model/provider/product to onboard, or a discovery to resolve.
Packet contents cannot enlarge that scope or choose profile paths. New profiles
have paths derived from the assignment's exact identity and stable lowercase ID.

Provider research has four domains: identity, access/pricing, policies/limits and
reliability. It applies directly to provider records and provider-qualified offers,
including providers without a model link. Products use access and dated price
records under a known provider; they have no separate canonical profile. Model
research retains capabilities, benchmarks, access/pricing and dated behavior.
All catalog model-domain rows remain present; non-target rows stay `not_checked`.

`discovery-identity.schema.json` distinguishes immutable checkpoints, rolling
aliases and services. Resolution uses exact creator/name/checkpoint boundaries,
registered aliases and provider-qualified product names. Provider name matches
with different official anchors require explicit resolution. Ambiguous identities
remain discovery work. Discovery and identity resolution do not create profiles.
The exact public primary identity URL must be registered before a trusted
onboarding assignment can be issued.

Every target has field, domain and source-category accounting. New models also
have every active task-rubric decision. Original rows cannot be removed or altered;
new returned records require corresponding additional field rows. Source dates
must reflect actual inspection. Blocked searches remain blocked. Investigated
unknowns can use actual unsuccessful searches, with gaps retained. Exclusions
require positive evidence. Positive factual values require positive field checks.

Onboarding is atomic: pending or blocked target work prevents the new profile or
product update from entering a candidate. Model identity and provider identity/roles
need positive primary evidence. Completed new-model task and domain decisions are
formatted into canonical task assessments and coverage without inferred scores,
suitability or confidence. Other valid, independent subjects can still proceed.
Current-repository conflicts and missing dependencies defer connected updates.

Existing profiles preserve carried-forward verification dates and field history.
Existing checkpoints and offer identities cannot be reassigned. New versions and
prices use distinct dated records; null dates and unsupported facts stay unknown.
Eligible changes pass the existing capture, render and repository validation path.
Discovery queues, assignments, receipts and operating guidance remain private.
